About Folder Structures

These folder structures define the Google Drive hierarchy that will be created for each company based on their entity type.

  • India entities: Use April-March financial year and include MCA, ROC, FEMA folders
  • US entities: Use Jan-Dec calendar year with incorporation and board document folders
  • Folders are created automatically when processing emails for new companies
  • To modify structures, edit entity_folder_structures.json
